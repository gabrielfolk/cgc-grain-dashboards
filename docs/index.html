About the data

  • Source: Canadian Grain Commission, Grain Statistics Weekly, crop years 2013-14 onward. Crop deep dives (uses, exports by port, stocks): Canola, Wheat, Durum, Barley. Feed use, livestock and feed prices: Western Feed Grains.
  • Producer deliveries are the sum of deliveries into licensed primary elevators, direct deliveries to processors, and producer-car shipments.
  • Weekly values are the week-over-week change in CGC's crop-year-to-date totals. CGC applies revisions only to those totals, so its own weekly rows add up 1–4% short. A revision can make a week negative.
  • Commercial stocks are week-end stocks in licensed country elevators, processors and port terminals. Grain stored on farms is not included. Ending stocks are the Jul 31 figure.
  • Figures cover Western Canadian grain. Wheat and corn graded Canada Eastern (CE grades, grown in Ontario and Quebec) are left out of exports and terminal stocks, so totals are lower than CGC's all-Canada figures. Soybean grades do not show origin, so soybean exports and stocks at eastern terminals may include Ontario and Quebec beans.
  • The 5-year average and range use the five crop years before this one, compared week for week.
  • Crop sizes are StatCan's production estimates for Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta and British Columbia (table 32-10-0359), the region CGC's weekly data covers. The current year uses StatCan's latest in-season estimate, which will be revised. Wheat excludes durum.
  • The full-year projection scales deliveries so far by the share of the year's deliveries that usually arrive by this week (5-year average). It is a simple pace estimate, not a forecast.
