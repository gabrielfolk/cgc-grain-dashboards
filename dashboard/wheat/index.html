    Country elevators

    Grain coming into licensed country elevators from farms against grain going out by rail and truck. When shipments lag deliveries, elevators fill up, which usually means rail or port congestion and weaker basis.

    Net build in country elevators

    Deliveries into country elevators minus shipments out, thousand tonnes since Aug 1. Rising = elevators filling.
